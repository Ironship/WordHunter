# Word Hunter 1.1.2

A fix release for 1.1.1. It repairs Wikipedia and Wikinews articles in
Discover, several kinds of PDF, EPUB and YouTube imports, restoring the backup
that the clear buttons make, and moving data between devices, along with a
number of reader and flashcard problems. Install it over 1.1.1 directly.

## Fixes

### Discover and importing

- **Wikipedia and Wikinews articles** — adding an article created a book that
  could never be opened. Articles now open as texts, and the broken entries
  that 1.1.0 and 1.1.1 saved are replaced when you open them or add them
  again. Results are listed by relevance, and the two sources are no longer
  offered for Ancient Greek (and Wikinews for Latin), where these wikis don't
  exist.
- **Gutenberg books from Discover** no longer add a second "(full text)" card
  to the library.
- **PDF files** saved with a browser's "Save as PDF" no longer come out with
  words split into pieces, and a PDF with a picture-only cover or blank pages
  is accepted when at least half of its pages have text.
- **EPUB books** with many pictures or an embedded font, which is common for
  Japanese and Chinese books, are no longer rejected as too large. A book that
  really is too large now says so.
- **The import panel** replaces the previous file's title, author and cover
  when you pick another file, lets you pick the same file again after a
  failure, recognizes more legacy text encodings, keeps subtitle lines that
  are only a number, and tells you when Calibre is missing.
- **On Android** the file picker offers Markdown and ASS/SSA subtitle files,
  and a file type that can't be imported gets a clear message.
- **YouTube subtitles** are found for more videos, the import no longer gives
  up while the download is still running, and the Snap package now includes
  a working yt-dlp.
- **Scanned PDFs and images** — OCR models you add for your language, as the
  [OCR model notes](https://github.com/Ironship/WordHunter/blob/main/src-tauri/ocr-runtime/README.md)
  describe, are now used, and a recognition model is always paired with its
  own character list.

### Reader

- Words after an elision in French and Italian (d', qu', j', dell', all' and
  the like) are saved as the word itself, Ukrainian words written with ʼ
  match the same word written with ', and Ancient Greek words with a grave
  accent match their acute form. Words you saved under the old forms are
  merged automatically.
- Selected phrases in French, Italian, German and Ancient Greek no longer
  look unsaved, phrases in bold or italic text are no longer glued together,
  and a new phrase changes color as soon as you set its status.
- German separable verbs spelled with ß are recognized.
- Ctrl+F and saved example sentences land on the right word in formatted
  texts, and bookmarks work with "All" words per page.
- Reading aloud on the desktop no longer reads the page buttons.
- Library statistics and the "in this text" word filter count Japanese,
  Chinese, Thai, Lao, Khmer and Burmese words the way the reader shows them.

### Words and flashcards

- A card graded in the reader is no longer asked again in the same flashcard
  session, and flashcards show the current translation.
- The review calendar keeps every day you reviewed a card, not just the last
  one, and keeps the review days of both devices when you sync.
- Deleting a word you have worked on asks for confirmation first.
- Typing 1 to 4 in the word editor's fields types the digit instead of
  changing the word's status.
- Graphs no longer count new words as due when the flashcards leave them out.
- "Download now" in the offline translator selects the missing language and
  installs it.

### Backups and moving data

- Restoring the backup that "Clear words", "Clear library" or "Clear
  everything" makes now brings back what was cleared.
- "Export everything" no longer contains your DeepL or AI keys, and importing
  a package from an older version no longer replaces this device's keys.
- Importing a package no longer deletes words or books on this device because
  they were deleted on the other one.
- A newly set up device takes the settings from the package and keeps its own
  bookmarks, and the bookmarks and archive state of the books a package
  brings come with them.
- A package with a PDF book whose page images are missing imports everything
  else and tells you that some books were skipped.
- Anki exports no longer turn the column names into a card.
- On Android, "Export everything" of a large library no longer fails after
  two minutes and shows its progress.

### The app

- The desktop app opens the book you were reading again after a restart,
  which 1.1.1 had broken.
- Reloading the app right after an edit no longer loses that edit.
- The desktop app starts faster with large scanned books.
- AI explanations accept Chinese and Russian text of the allowed length,
  DeepL explains that it has no Latin, LM Studio gets more time to answer,
  and offline translation no longer stops in the middle of a paragraph.
- Picture search for Ancient Greek searches the Greek Wikipedia.
- "See what's new" in the update dialog opens the browser on the desktop.
- The app's built-in server gives book pictures and page scans only to the
  app, and opens links only when the app asks.

## Known issues

- Scanned PDFs and images in languages other than Chinese and English are
  read with the bundled Chinese and English model, which can miss letters
  such as ä, ł or Cyrillic. You can add a model for your language as the OCR
  model notes describe.
- When you show the Upcoming reviews list under the flashcards, it still
  lists today's cards in the order they will come.

## Upgrade notes

- The first start after the update merges words saved under the old forms
  described above. Library statistics for Japanese, Chinese, Thai, Lao, Khmer
  and Burmese books are counted again once.
