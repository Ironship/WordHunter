# Word Hunter 1.1.3

A small fix release for 1.1.2. Word translations now follow the language you
translate into when you change it. Install it over 1.1.2 directly.

## Fixes

- **Word translations after changing your language** — words you had already
  opened kept the translation made into your previous language. If you started
  in English and switched to Polish, for example, those words stayed in
  English while new words and sentence translations came out in Polish. Now a
  translation the app made for you is made again in your current language the
  next time you open the word. Translations you typed or imported yourself are
  never replaced.
- A word translation that was still on its way when you changed the language
  is no longer saved in the old language.

## Known issues

- Flashcards and the word list show the new translation once you have opened
  the word in the reader again.
- Scanned PDFs and images in languages other than Chinese and English are
  read with the bundled Chinese and English model, which can miss letters
  such as ä, ł or Cyrillic. You can add a model for your language as the
  [OCR model notes](https://github.com/Ironship/WordHunter/blob/main/src-tauri/ocr-runtime/README.md)
  describe.

## Upgrade notes

- Words translated automatically before this release don't record which
  language they were translated into, so each is translated once more the
  first time you open it after the update.
