# Word Hunter 1.1.6

A small fix release for 1.1.5. Reviewing flashcards past midnight now keeps
the card you were looking at when you reveal its answer. Install it over
1.1.5 directly.

## Fixes

- **Flashcards across midnight** — if you opened a flashcard just before
  midnight and clicked **Show** after midnight, the deck reshuffled for the
  new day and displayed the answer for a different word. The current card now
  stays on screen so **Show** always reveals its own answer, while cards that
  become due on the new day are added to the end of the session.
- **Switching flashcards or profiles with an open answer** — if the active
  card changes while an answer is open, the answer is now hidden instead of
  revealing the next card right away.

## Known issues

- Scanned PDFs and images in languages other than Chinese and English are
  read with the bundled Chinese and English model, which can miss letters
  such as ä, ł or Cyrillic. You can add a model for your language as the
  [OCR model notes](https://github.com/Ironship/WordHunter/blob/main/src-tauri/ocr-runtime/README.md)
  describe.
