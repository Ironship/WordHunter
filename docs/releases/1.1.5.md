# Word Hunter 1.1.5

A small fix release for 1.1.4. The Reader now keeps your place in a text.
Install it over 1.1.4 directly.

## Fixes

- **Reading position** — opening a text from its card in the library saved
  your place as the top of the page just before the text appeared, so you
  started from the top every time. Your place is now kept, on every
  platform.
- **Word Hunter Pocket after a restart** — a text you imported yourself, such
  as a Wikipedia article, was not ready yet when the app started, so the app
  opened the library instead and the text then lost its place. The text you
  were reading now opens again where you left it, with a short loading
  indicator while your library loads.
- **Small shift on every reopen** — each time a text was opened again, the
  place moved back about half a screen. It now stays put.
- The Reader no longer shows the generic "Reader" title instead of the text's
  title when the app's language differs from the system's.

## Known issues

- Scanned PDFs and images in languages other than Chinese and English are
  read with the bundled Chinese and English model, which can miss letters
  such as ä, ł or Cyrillic. You can add a model for your language as the
  [OCR model notes](https://github.com/Ironship/WordHunter/blob/main/src-tauri/ocr-runtime/README.md)
  describe.
