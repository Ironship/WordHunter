# Word Hunter 1.1.1

A small update to 1.1.0 for two things people reported after it shipped: the
desktop app opening with the wrong languages and an empty library, and the
flashcard queue giving away the next cards. Install it over 1.1.0 directly.

## Fixes

- **The desktop app opens with your own languages and books** — since 1.1.0
  the window could first show English and German with no imported books until
  your library finished loading, and a language picked during that time was
  undone. Word Hunter now opens straight into your saved language pair, theme
  and library.
- **Flashcards no longer show what comes next** — the Upcoming reviews list
  under the card followed the order of today's cards, so it revealed the next
  words even after shuffling. It is now collapsed by default. Click its heading
  to show or hide it; Word Hunter remembers your choice.

## Known issues

- When you show the Upcoming reviews list, it still lists today's cards in the
  order they will come.

## Upgrade notes

- The Upcoming reviews list starts collapsed, including for people who used
  it in 1.1.0. One click on its heading brings it back.
- With a very large library, the desktop window appears once the library has
  loaded, as it did before 1.1.0.
