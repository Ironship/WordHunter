What is new in Word Hunter 1.0.12-rc.1:

- Android reliability: minSdk raised to Android 8.0 (API 26), day/night theme support, background-service watchdog, improved file-open flows
- Renderer migration continues: settings, import, library, reader and translator panels are now TypeScript renderers
- Redesigned Argos download dialog
- Hardened release CI: AAB manifests are now parsed directly (aapt2 incompatibility with AGP 8.11 bundles)
