Word Hunter 1.0.9-rc.11

- Adds a 0–100% progress bar to desktop package export, with stage labels
  and a background export job so the UI no longer freezes.
- Speeds up PC shutdown by skipping a redundant full save when nothing
  changed and using a non-blocking keepalive flush.
- Android UX: PDF import shows per-page progress and a cancel button,
  word-sheet pull gestures expand/collapse the card, landscape phones keep
  the Pocket layout, startup renders from the UI cache immediately, and
  continuous TTS pauses/resumes when the app goes to the background.
